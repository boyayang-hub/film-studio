3Film

Check it in the preview. Then Record opens the film in its own tab: choose "This tab" when Chrome asks what to share, and don't touch anything until it ends. The video downloads by itself (MP4 in recent Chrome).

Tips for a clean recording

Use Chrome on a 1920×1080 (or larger) screen, close other tabs, plug in the laptop. The film plays full screen from the start and the video is the film's own sound, so system noises are not recorded. If a recording stutters, record again with fewer apps open.
